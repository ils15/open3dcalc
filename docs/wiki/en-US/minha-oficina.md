# My Workshop

**My Workshop** is your operation's full registry in one place: printers,
materials, spool stock and sales channels. It replaces the old separate tabs
(registry, supplies and the browse-only library) — instead of three loose
lists you get **one global registry with "Mine" and "Library" collections**.

## The four sections

Each section has a **Mine | Library** switch:

- **Mine** — what you registered: your printers, your materials, your spools
  and your sales channels with the fees you negotiated.
- **Library** — the catalog that ships with the app: 103 printer models with
  images and specifications, reference materials and fee profiles for the
  marketplaces (Shopee, Mercado Livre, Amazon, Shein, TikTok Shop, Magalu,
  Etsy and others).

**The library is never deleted by your edits** — it is the reference base. To
use one of its items, open the library and tap **Select** or **Create editable
copy**: the item lands in "Mine" and you adjust the numbers to your reality.
Empty personal lists show a shortcut straight to the library.

## Printers

Each printer stores power, acquisition cost, useful life and maintenance per
hour — the numbers the calculator uses for depreciation and energy. The
library covers the main brands (Bambu Lab, Creality, Anycubic, Prusa, Elegoo
and others) with photos or brand cards.

## Materials

Density and average price per type (FDM and resin). The price you register in
"My materials" is the price **you** pay — the library is a sanity check, not
the truth.

## Stock

The stock view has two shapes, switched at the top:

- **Management** — the full view: value and weight KPIs, filters by material
  and status, a spool form with real filament brands (one tap fills the
  brand; the suggested price range helps you sanity-check the cost/kg).
- **Shelf** — the compact card view, with the remaining-filament bar on each
  spool.

## Stores & sales channels

Each channel stores the **percentage fee** and the **fixed per-unit fee** the
marketplace charges. The calculator uses those numbers for the sell price and
the per-channel profit comparison. Custom channels land in "Mine"; catalog
profiles stay in the library.
